How Reliable Are Bangla Misogyny Detectors? A Behavioral Test Suite in Bangla and Banglish

BRAC University · NLP and Machine Learning Research · Ongoing

Research objective: To develop a functional test suite for evaluating the reliability, robustness, and generalization of Bangla misogyny detection models beyond conventional held-out evaluation metrics.

Research questions: The study investigates whether standard test scores overestimate real-world reliability, which linguistic phenomena expose model weaknesses, whether Banglish reduces performance relative to Bangla script, and how well models generalize across datasets.

Dataset and test suite: OMANISHA is the proposed primary dataset, with misogyny- related categories mapped to a documented binary classification scheme. Cross-dataset evaluation using suitable public Bangla data, such as ComMA, is planned subject to licensing, availability, and label compatibility. The proposed functional test suite will contain 10–12 linguistic functionalities and 150–300 minimal pairs, with parallel Bangla and Banglish versions.

Models and methodology: A TF-IDF character and word n-gram baseline with Logistic Regression will be compared with BanglaBERT, XLM-RoBERTa, and MuRIL.

Evaluation: Accuracy, precision, recall, macro-F1, confusion matrices, minimal-pair consistency, Bangla–Banglish performance gaps, and cross-dataset will be examined.

Expected contribution: A bilingual-script behavioral test suite, an analysis of model failure modes and dataset shortcuts in low-resource and code-mixed language settings.

How Reliable Are Bangla Misogyny Detectors? A Behavioral Test Suite in Bangla and Banglish

BRAC University · NLP / Machine Learning Course Project · In Progress

Online misogyny is common on Bangla social media, and several Bangla datasets now exist, such as OMANISHA, with 7,017 annotated Bengali text samples collected from Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Twitter and news portals. Most detectors are judged only by accuracy or F1 on a held-out split. Held-out scores can overestimate real performance because datasets contain biases and shortcuts, such as relying on slur keywords instead of meaning.

Baseline TF-IDF (character and word n-grams) + Logistic Regression. Transformer Models BanglaBERT XLM-RoBERTa MuRIL (optionally BanglishBERT)
