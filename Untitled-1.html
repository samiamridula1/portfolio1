Ongoing Research: Bangla-Banglish Sexism Detection Using NLP

BRAC University - NLP / Machine Learning Course Project - In Progress

Studying sexist and gender-based harassment content in low-resource, code-mixed Bangla and Banglish text. Building a small annotated dataset (500-1000 public comments, labeled Sexist / Not Sexist and by script type: Bangla, Banglish, or Mixed) with duplicate and personal-identifier removal.

Planned evaluation compares a TF-IDF + Logistic Regression baseline with BanglaBERT and XLM-RoBERTa using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and confusion matrices, with a focus on how script variation affects performance.

Undergraduate Research: Online Gaming and Gambling Tendencies

BRAC University - HCI & Mixed-Methods Research

Investigated gambling-like tendencies linked to chance-based reward mechanics in online games among Bangladeshi youth. Conducted 30 semi-structured interviews and analyzed survey responses from 150 participants using thematic and statistical analysis.

Designed two responsible-gaming prototypes with spending-management controls and a reward-progress indicator, then evaluated them through usability testing with 10 participants.
